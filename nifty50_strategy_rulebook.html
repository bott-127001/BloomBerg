00What this document covers

This rulebook defines the complete signal generation pipeline — from raw OHLCV data to a single actionable trade per day. It is structured as three sequential modules that must be evaluated in order every morning before market open.

ModulePurposeDecision output
Module 1Volatility regime detectionWhich threshold set to use today
Module 25-filter signal scanList of qualifying stocks for today
Module 31-trade selection + executionOne stock, direction, TP, SL
Hard rule
A module's output is the required input for the next module. No filter in Module 2 can be evaluated before Module 1 sets the regime. No trade can be entered before all 5 filters in Module 2 pass.

Timing dependency

02Glossary of metrics

Every metric used in this strategy is defined here. All are computed from daily OHLCV or intraday candle data only — no external data feed required except India VIX.

Vol(9:15) — Volume at the 9:15 candle

The total traded volume recorded in the first 5-minute candle of the day (9:15–9:19:59).

Vol(9:15) = Volume field of the 9:15 candle for the stock

Vol_MA(10) — 10-day volume moving average

The average of the total daily volume for the stock over the past 10 trading sessions. This is the baseline "normal" daily volume.

Vol_MA(10) = ( Sum of daily volume for each of the last 10 trading days ) ÷ 10
// Uses full-day volume from daily candles, not intraday candles

Vol_Ratio — Volume surge ratio

How many times larger today's 9:15 volume is compared to the stock's average daily volume. A ratio above 1.5 means unusually heavy participation at open.

Vol_Ratio = Vol(9:15) ÷ Vol_MA(10)

ATR(5) — Average True Range over 5 days

The average of the True Range over the last 5 trading sessions. It measures how much the stock typically moves each day in percentage terms. Used to gauge the stock's recent volatility regime.

True Range (each day) = Maximum of: ( High − Low ), ( |High − Previous Close| ), ( |Low − Previous Close| ) ATR(5) = ( Sum of True Range for each of last 5 days ) ÷ 5 ATR(5)% = ( ATR(5) ÷ Previous Close ) × 100
// Always express ATR as a percentage of price, not in absolute rupee terms

Gap% — Opening gap percentage

How much the stock opened above or below its previous day's close, as a percentage. A positive gap means the stock opened higher (bullish gap). A negative gap means it opened lower (bearish gap).

Gap% = ( Today's Open − Previous Close ) ÷ Previous Close × 100
// Positive = gap up (long candidate). Negative = gap down (short candidate).

Gap/ATR Ratio — Conviction score

How large the gap is relative to the stock's own normal daily range. This is the selector metric used to pick the best trade on days with multiple qualifying stocks.

Gap_ATR_Ratio = |Gap%| ÷ ATR(5)%
// Use absolute value of Gap% — direction is handled separately
// Higher ratio = gap is more abnormal relative to the stock's own behavior

Nifty_MA(20) — Nifty 50 index 20-day moving average

The simple moving average of the Nifty 50 index daily close over the past 20 trading sessions. Used to determine the broad market direction.

Nifty_MA(20) = ( Sum of Nifty 50 daily close for each of last 20 sessions ) ÷ 20

Nifty direction

Whether the Nifty 50 index is bullish or bearish at the open of the current day.

If Nifty 9:15 Open > Nifty_MA(20) → Nifty direction = BULLISH If Nifty 9:15 Open < Nifty_MA(20) → Nifty direction = BEARISH If equal → No trade today (skip)

9:15 candle direction

Whether the first 5-minute candle of the day closed higher or lower than it opened. This determines whether the initial impulse was bullish or bearish.

If 9:15 Close > 9:15 Open → Candle direction = BULLISH If 9:15 Close < 9:15 Open → Candle direction = BEARISH If equal (doji) → Skip this stock

9:20 candle confirmation

Whether the second 5-minute candle (9:20–9:24:59) continues in the same direction as the gap. This is the final fakeout filter before entry.

For a LONG setup (gap up): If 9:20 Close > 9:20 Open → Confirmed. Enter long at 9:20 candle close. If 9:20 Close < 9:20 Open → Not confirmed. Skip this stock. For a SHORT setup (gap down): If 9:20 Close < 9:20 Open → Confirmed. Enter short at 9:20 candle close. If 9:20 Close > 9:20 Open → Not confirmed. Skip this stock.

03Module 1 — Volatility regime detection

Before any scan runs, determine which threshold set to use. This is a once-per-day decision made before market open using India VIX. There are two threshold sets — High Vol (2024 thresholds) and Low Vol (2025 thresholds). The regime decision dictates the ATR entry filter and the profit target used in Module 3.

Step 1 — Read India VIX

Check India VIX before 9:15 AM. It is available on the NSE website (nseindia.com), any broker terminal, or TradingView (symbol: NSE:INDIAVIX). Use the previous day's closing value if live pre-market value is unavailable.

VIX below 15 — Low vol
Regime: Low volatility
Use: 2025 thresholds
Market is calm. Gaps are smaller and targets must be conservative.
VIX 15 to 20 — Transition
Regime: Ambiguous
Use: ATR scan average
VIX is not decisive. Resolve by computing the avg ATR(5) from your scan stocks. See step below.
VIX above 20 — High vol
Regime: High volatility
Use: 2024 thresholds
Market is active. Gaps have strong follow-through. Use wider TP.

Step 2 — ATR tiebreaker (only when VIX is 15 to 20)

When India VIX is in the 15–20 range, VIX alone is not reliable enough to classify the regime. In this case, compute the average ATR(5) across all Nifty 50 stocks that have already passed Filter 2 (ATR ≥ the minimum threshold) in your scan. This gives you the actual volatility reading of the stocks you are considering trading today.

How to compute avg ATR for the tiebreaker
After running your pre-open scan on the Nifty 50 universe and computing ATR(5)% for every stock, collect the ATR(5)% values of all stocks that have passed at least Filter 2. Average them. This number — the average ATR of your shortlist — is your tiebreaker signal.
Avg_ATR_Scan = Sum of ATR(5)% for each stock in the shortlist ÷ Count of stocks in shortlist If Avg_ATR_Scan ≥ 2.5% → High vol regime → Use 2024 thresholds If Avg_ATR_Scan < 2.5% → Low vol regime → Use 2025 thresholds
Why 2.5% and not 2.6%
The sensitivity analysis showed that 2.5% captures 100% of 2024 (high vol) trading days while still classifying 71% of 2025 (low vol) days correctly. It avoids ever misclassifying a high-vol day as low-vol, which is the more expensive error (you would use conservative 2025 targets in a volatile market and leave money on the table).

Regime threshold reference

Profit target (TP)+1.25%+1.0%

04Module 2 — 5-filter signal scan

Run this scan on every Nifty 50 constituent stock after 9:15 AM candle closes. All 5 filters must pass for a stock to qualify. A single failure removes the stock. The filters are evaluated in order — stop evaluating a stock the moment any filter fails.

Important
Filters 1, 2, and 3 can be computed before market open using pre-market data where available, or from the 9:15 candle once it closes. Filter 4 requires the 9:15 candle close. Filter 5 requires the 9:20 candle close. Entry happens after Filter 5 passes.
1
Volume surge — unusual participation at open
pre-open

Definition

Confirms that the trading activity at open is genuinely elevated — not routine morning noise. A ratio of 1.5× means today's open volume is 50% heavier than the stock's 10-day average daily volume, which signals institutional or informed participation.

Calculation

Vol_MA(10) = Average of daily total volume across last 10 sessions Vol_Ratio = Vol(9:15 candle) ÷ Vol_MA(10)

Pass condition

PASS if Vol_Ratio ≥ 1.5 FAIL if Vol_Ratio < 1.5 → Remove stock, move to next
Data note
Vol_MA(10) uses the full-day volume from daily candles. Vol(9:15) is the volume field from the first 5-minute candle. These are different time scales — do not mix them with intraday bars.
2
ATR regime — stock is in a volatile state
pre-open

Definition

Ensures the stock has been making meaningful daily moves over the last 5 sessions. A low-ATR stock in a quiet period will not have the range needed for the gap to follow through to the target. The ATR threshold changes depending on the regime set in Module 1.

Calculation

For each of the last 5 trading days: TR_day = max( High−Low, |High−Prev_Close|, |Low−Prev_Close| ) ATR(5)_points = ( TR_1 + TR_2 + TR_3 + TR_4 + TR_5 ) ÷ 5 ATR(5)% = ( ATR(5)_points ÷ Previous Close ) × 100

Pass condition

High vol regime → PASS if ATR(5)% ≥ 2.5% Low vol regime → PASS if ATR(5)% ≥ 1.75% FAIL = ATR(5)% below the applicable threshold → Remove stock
3
Gap size — real directional commitment at open
pre-open

Definition

Filters out stocks that opened flat or nearly flat. A meaningful gap indicates that buyers or sellers have already committed to a direction overnight. Stocks that gap by less than 0.5% from the previous close are not making a directional statement — they are just opening in noise.

Calculation

Gap% = ( Today's Open − Previous Close ) ÷ Previous Close × 100
// Positive value = gap up. Negative value = gap down.
// Previous Close = the last 4pm close of the stock, not the Nifty index.

Pass condition

PASS if |Gap%| ≥ 0.5% (absolute value — applies to both gap up and gap down) FAIL if |Gap%| < 0.5% → Remove stock Gap direction: Gap% > 0 → Long candidate Gap% < 0 → Short candidate
4
Triple alignment — all three directions must agree
after 9:15 candle

Definition

The single biggest win-rate driver in the strategy. Requires that three independent signals — the stock's gap direction, the 9:15 candle direction, and the broad Nifty market direction — all point the same way. Any conflict between them kills the trade.

The three signals

SignalBullish conditionBearish condition
Gap directionGap% > 0 (opened above prev close)Gap% < 0 (opened below prev close)
9:15 candle direction9:15 Close > 9:15 Open9:15 Close < 9:15 Open
Nifty vs MA(20)Nifty 9:15 Open > Nifty_MA(20)Nifty 9:15 Open < Nifty_MA(20)

Pass condition

Long signal PASS: Gap direction = BULLISH AND 9:15 candle direction = BULLISH AND Nifty vs MA(20) = BULLISH Short signal PASS: Gap direction = BEARISH AND 9:15 candle direction = BEARISH AND Nifty vs MA(20) = BEARISH
FAIL if any one of the three signals conflicts with the other two → Skip this stock entirely. Do not enter even if the other two agree.
Doji candles
If the 9:15 candle is a doji (Close = Open exactly), it has no direction. Treat it as a conflict — skip the stock.
5
9:20 candle confirmation — direction holds for one full candle
after 9:20 candle

Definition

The final filter before entry. Many gaps reverse sharply in the first 5 minutes as trapped traders exit. This filter waits for the second 5-minute candle to close. If the direction still holds, the move has momentum and is less likely to be a fakeout. If the second candle reverses, skip the stock for today — do not chase.

Calculation

Wait for the 9:20–9:24:59 candle to fully close (at exactly 9:25:00 AM). 9:20 candle direction: If Close > Open → BULLISH If Close < Open → BEARISH

Pass condition

Long setup → PASS if 9:20 candle is BULLISH (Close > Open) Short setup → PASS if 9:20 candle is BEARISH (Close < Open) FAIL = 9:20 candle opposes gap direction → Skip stock entirely
Entry timing
Entry is placed at the close of the 9:20 candle — i.e., at 9:25:00 AM. This is a market order at open of the 9:25 candle, or a limit order at the 9:20 close price. Never enter during the 9:20 candle while it is still forming.

05Module 3 — 1-trade-per-day selection

After Module 2 produces a list of qualifying stocks, exactly one trade is taken per day. The selector picks the highest-conviction candidate using the Gap/ATR Ratio.

Why Gap/ATR Ratio

Among all tested selectors (vol ratio highest, ATR highest, gap% largest, and their inverses), Gap/ATR Ratio descending produced the best risk-adjusted result: 54.1% win rate, +73.2% total PnL, and the lowest max drawdown of −3.64% across 181 trades.

The logic: a 1% gap on a stock with ATR of 2% is 50% of its entire normal daily range — an extraordinary move. The same 1% gap on a stock with ATR of 5% is only 20% of its range — routine. The ratio normalises for each stock's own volatility, selecting the gap that is most abnormal relative to that stock's own history.

Selection rule

For each qualifying stock from Module 2: Gap_ATR_Ratio = |Gap%| ÷ ATR(5)% Sort qualifying stocks by Gap_ATR_Ratio — highest first. Take the stock at rank 1 (highest ratio) as today's trade. All other qualifying stocks are skipped for today.
When no stocks qualify
If Module 2 produces zero qualifying stocks after all 5 filters, there is no trade today. Do not force a trade. The system is designed to sit in cash on low-signal days.

06Entry, profit target and stop loss

Entry price

Entry_Price = Close price of the 9:20 candle (9:24:59 close)
// Place order at market open of 9:25 candle, or as a limit at the 9:20 close

Profit target (TP)

Long trade: TP = Entry_Price × ( 1 + TP_pct ÷ 100 ) Short trade: TP = Entry_Price × ( 1 − TP_pct ÷ 100 ) Where TP_pct: High vol regime → 1.25% Low vol regime → 1.0%

Stop loss (SL)

Long trade: SL = Entry_Price × ( 1 − 0.75 ÷ 100 ) = Entry_Price × 0.9925 Short trade: SL = Entry_Price × ( 1 + 0.75 ÷ 100 ) = Entry_Price × 1.0075 SL is fixed at 0.75% in both regimes.

Exit rules

  • Exit 1
    Target hit — Close position immediately when price touches TP. Do not hold for more.
  • Exit 2
    Stop hit — Close position immediately when price touches SL. Do not average down or widen stop.
  • Exit 3
    End of day — If neither TP nor SL is hit by 3:20 PM, close the position at market price before EOD. Never carry intraday positions overnight.
